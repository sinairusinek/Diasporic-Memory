Your grandfathers Sally Wormser and Adolf Wormser

Dear Mrs. Vaädi,

much to our regret we have not many information about your grandfathers.

Sally Wormser, born Dec. 10, 1873 at Amsterdam as son of Max Wormser (1833-
1884), died Nov. 3, 1920 at Düsseldorf and was buried Nov. 11 at Frankfurt, cemetery
Rat-Beil-Straße. He was director of Weilwerke GmbH, a factory of bicycles and
typewriters at Frankfurt-Rödelheim. His wife was Lisa née Simon; we do not know her
birth and death dates.

Adolf Gerson, born Sept. 17, 1876 at Schwedt/Oder (Brandenburg), died or was killed
July 17, 1943 at Auschwitz; he was buried Oct. 6, 1943 at Frankfurt, cemetery
Eckenheimer Landstraße.
