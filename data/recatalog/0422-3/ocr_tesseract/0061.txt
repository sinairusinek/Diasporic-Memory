Hitler and the Nazis did not originate the belief that Jewish blood accounts
for Jewish personal qualities. Nicholas Slonimsky, described by the New York
Times as "one of the world's foremost musical lexicographers", published his
autobiography in 1988.° The 93-year-old Mr. Slonimsky wrote that, at the age of
15, he made the "traumatic discovery" that his family were converted Jews. His
aunt, Isabelle Vengerova, a celebrated pianist, accidentally revealed to the nephew
the Jewish origin of the family. The boy's outraged mother wrote to Vengerova
that "no outsider has the moral right to tell innocent children that their blood is
tainted by syphilitic infection. It is the same with Jewish blood." This was written

in 1909 when Hitler was merely 10 years old.
