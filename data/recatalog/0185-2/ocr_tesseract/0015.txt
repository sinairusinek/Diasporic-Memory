Berlin 1936 and 1994

This story has its beginnings in Berlin and ends in Berlin.

In 1936 my parents gave me a trip to Berlin to meet my mother's relatives and to see the
city. I was the guest of my aunt Emma, my mother's sister. Somehow this aunt was also
related by marriage to my father's side of the family. By coincident my cousin, Rosel
Birnbrey (Rachel Sharon) also visited there at the same time.

We were both approximately the same age and very anxious to impress one another and to
share family secrets. I recall sitting on a piano bench with Rachel as she told me that she
had discovered that our fathers had a third brother. Apparently they had fallen out with this
brother, as some times happens in families, and simply never discussed him with us.

According to Rachel's story, she and a group of girls were walking home from school when
they passed a slightly crippled man. As kids will often do, especially when they are with a
group of their peers, they poked fun at this man's plight.

When Rachel came home, and told her mother what had taken place, at that point, and based
on Rachel's description of this man, Rachel's mother sat her down and explained that this
person was really her father's brother.

Until his death a year later, we never discussed this uncle and of course I left my parents’
home a few months later, and consequently this was a gap in my family history which I never
fully understood.

When we started to make a family tree, Rachel did know his name and his name has been
on our family tree ever since. This man was my father's brother, Hermann Birnbrei. Until
1994 I spelled his last name the same way we spell our name.

This year, while on a visit to Berlin, Shirlye perused the Berlin telephone directory and ran
across a Zilla Birnbrei. Since this was the closest entry to our name which we had ever
encountered, we immediately placed a phone call to this Zilla Birnbrei.

I explained the nature of my call, where my father came from etc. and there was immediate
recognition.
