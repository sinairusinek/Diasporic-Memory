Lucas, Weiss, Mibashan — political and personal papers

Folder · 0009-002 · 19 items · 1903–1948

Folder of political and personal papers connected to Heinz Bock, Walter Lucas and the Mibashan family. Contains identity documents, Berlin-period correspondence, and materials from the years of forced migration and internment in Sachsenhausen, Kitchener Camp, and the Donera transport to Australia.
