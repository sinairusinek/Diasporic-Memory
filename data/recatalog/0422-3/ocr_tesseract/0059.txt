Myths explain reality through the use of imagination. The center of mythical

cosmology in Western thought has been Jerusalem. Empirical interpretation of
phenomena had its beginnings in Athens. The Greek culture had an elaborate
mythical structure which gave way to Scientific cosmology. The former explained
thunder as caused by Zeus shaking his aegis in anger, the latter gave as a reason
for thunder, the escape of wind from the clouds. Present views of the cause of
thunder are merely an improvement upon the empirical wind theory of the ancient
Greeks. The Ptolemaic system and the Copernican view of the universe are
philosophically of the same variety; they are empirical in nature.
