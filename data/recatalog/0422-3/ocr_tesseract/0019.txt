A Poland Catholic or Else Not Polish?

Roman Catholics had never enjoyed a monopoly in the religious affairs of the old Polish
Commonwealth. Although they were the largest single religious group, in 1772 they constituted
significantly less than half, only 43%, of the population.2? Pre-partitioned Poland had always
been a multi-ethnic, multi-religious, what we would call today a pluralist, society. Along with
Roman Catholics there had always been first pagans, then Jews, Orthodox Ukrainians and Uniate
Ruthenians, Hussites, Lutherans, Calvinists, Socinian Unitarians, even Muslims descended from

the Tartars.
