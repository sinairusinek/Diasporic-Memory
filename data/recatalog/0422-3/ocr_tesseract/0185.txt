The Annual Scholars' Conference on the Holocaust and the Churches was
founded in 1970 by Franklin H. Littell and Hubert G. Locke as an
interfaith, interdisciplinary and international organization. Devoted to
remembering and learning from the Holocaust, and encouraging the
participation of both educators and local communities, its mission is to

promote scholarly research that examines issues raised by the "Final
Solution."
