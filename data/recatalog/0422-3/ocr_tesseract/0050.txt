The Age of Enlightenment brought with it the re-emergence of secular anti-
Judaism, i.e., antisemitism.

"Instead of disappearing with the Enlightenment, antisemitism simply
found a new guise, one which no longer blamed the Jews for the
crucifixion of Christ but held them responsible for all the crimes and
perversities committed in the name of monotheistic religion; the
Jews were no longer guilty of rejecting Christian belief but were
judged to be inherently perverse, and their ‘fossilized’ religion to be
an obstacle to human progress. In the arch-sceptic Voltaire the
resulting image of the Jew is one of utter scom and contempt. The
Old Testament is ridiculed and calumnied as a compendium of
cannibalism, folly and error. The Jews were caricatured as 'the most
imbecile people on the face of the earth’, as ‘obtuse, cruel and
absurd’, the heirs of a history that was both ‘disgusting and
abominable'.""
