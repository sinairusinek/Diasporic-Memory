Many of my relatives died in the Holocaust. We don't want them to be forgotten, and we, the
survivors, are the only ones who can bear witness. These were people who had dreams,
people who wanted to contribute to society and who thought they had the right to raise

families and live lives of normalcy and peace.

When I list the people who perished in the Holocaust, one has to remember that these were
the only ones whose fate I found recorded, the ones not listed, are awaiting my continuing
search, or perhaps, your continuing search. I plan to update these records constantly as I

continue to gather facts and information.
