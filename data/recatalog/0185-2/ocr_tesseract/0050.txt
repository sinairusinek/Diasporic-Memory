Notes and Explanation of Symbols

Numbers Numbers in front of names on Descendants’ Tree refer to number of generations

away from first person listed on tree.

b. abbreviation for "date of birth"
m. abbreviation for "date of marriage"
d. abbreviation for "date of death"

The year following each name has no meaning. Please look only for the dates shown

following the letters listed above.
It was not possible to list siblings according to age as all dates of birth were not available.

It is also possible that not all siblings are listed as I only had limited information on some
families

Since going to press we have engaged the services of RAGAS, a quasi governmental
organization engaged in genealogical research in the former Soviet Union to obtain additional
information on the Kresses/Cohen families.

Your input and corrections will be appreciated so that the family trees can be pdated
periodically as more information becomes available. :
