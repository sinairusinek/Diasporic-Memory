Religion is a social phenomenon. It requires symbolic communication. The
crucifixion of Christ is a symbol of human suffering. A Christian theologian,
Franklin H. Littell, used this same symbol for Jewish suffering when he wrote the
book entitled The Crucifixion of the Jews. Professor Littell's book was published
in 1975. In 1938, Mark Chagal, a Jewish artist, completed a painting called "White
Crucifixion". Karl Plank, a professor of religion at Davidson College in North
Carolina, described the painting:
