The central theme of Christology is not crucifixion but resurrection. To be
crucified in Roman times was not a mark of divinity, resurrection was.
Resurrection redeemed the crucifixion according to the New Testament. The
Holocaust was a crucifixion without redemption. The Holocaust took us back to
Golgatha.

Wesel, particularly his poetic memoir "Night". Wiesel's message in "Night" is that
God was silent at Auschwitz. Francois Mauriac (1885-1970), the famous Catholic
novelist, and Elie Wesel, the future bard of the Holocaust, met in 1954. At that
point, Wiesel was an unknown journalist, Mauriac, a Nobel Laureate in literature.
