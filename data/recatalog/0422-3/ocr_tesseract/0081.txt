B: General
"Antisemitism"

Why did Mauriac call a Hungarian Jew living in Paris an Israeli? Could it
be that, in Mauriac's mind, creation of the state of Israel transformed all Jews into
Israelis? Legally, every Jew is potentially a citizen of Israel but that does not make
him an Israeli. Could it be that Mauriac recognized that after the Holocaust, the
term "Jew' lost its meaning? We will never know for sure. Mauriac, like the

European Jewry, is dead.
