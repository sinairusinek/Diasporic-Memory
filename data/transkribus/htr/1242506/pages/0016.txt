Many Germans Jews have received invitations from their place
of birth in Germany, of where they had been active, at the expense of
their hosts: Flights, hotels, excursions, visits to theatres,
concerts, the lot. Strangely enough many people have accepted this
kind of atonement. I could never do that. Pride forbids it. Never
could I go back to where I had been a happy child with my parents, and
my grandparents. It would simply break my heart.
