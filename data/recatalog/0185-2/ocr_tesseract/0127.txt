THE BIRNBREY/LEVY FAMILY

It is ironical, when I began my search for family, I thought that the Jacobsohn family was big
and the Birnbrey family relatively small. Additionally, I knew of no survivors in the Birnbrey
family, and now find that this family has a number of survivors, though certainly not what it
should have been had it not been for the Holocaust. I am merely speaking in relative terms.

It is also ironical that I knew much less about the Birnbrey Family than I did about the
Jacobsohn Family, and I will try to explain why.

I had been told that the Birnbrey family lived in Stettin, Pommern, (Pomerania) for many
generations (Stettin is now known as Szczecin, Poland). My grandparents died at a relatively
young age, my grandfather was Magnus Birnbrey (after whom | received my Hebrew name
Menashe), and my grandmother was Auguste Levy. It appears that she had five brothers and
sisters and one of her sisters married a Gustav Gerson, and the Gerson's made the largest
addition, in numbers, to this family tree.

In researching the available documents from the Stettin community 1 was unable to find our
family names on those synagogue memberships which were preserved in the archives of the
Mormon Church. The only item of interest and trivia I found is that in 1827 Felix
Mendelssohn personally conducted the premiere of his composition "A Midsummer Night's
Dream" in a concert hall which seemed to be across the street from the family home on
Schiitzengarten.

Subsequently I located some records from an archive which changed several of the
understandings which I always thought I "knew" about my family. On some documents the
name Birnbrey was spelled Birnbrei, consequently | am in the dark how, why and when the
name was changed. Much to my surprise, my grandfather used his Hebrew name Manasse
(Menashe) and only later the German name Magnus. He died December 20, 1900 at age 50.
He was not born in Stettin as I had surmised, he was born in Gratz. My grandmother was
born in Berkenbriigge, Kreis Arnswalde on June 10, 1849 and died March 30, 1922. On my
father's birth certificate her place of birth is shown as Stettin. There is another inconsistency,
as the death certificate gives her age as 71 at time of death. My grandparents were married
on July 2, 1879 in Stettin. If I am reading the documents correctly, my grandmother was a
widow at the time of her marriage to my grandfather.

My great grandfather was Laiser Birnbrei, who was a horse trader and his wife was Sara Bik
Birnbrei. Both of them were deceased at the time of the marriage of my grandparents.
