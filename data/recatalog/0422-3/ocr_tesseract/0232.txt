Iv. Final Thoughts

Post-Shoah re-reading Biblical texts, both Torah and New
Testament, because of the Shoah, marks a new and significant
direction in Jewish-Christian relations. Out of the crucible
of our common pain and all-too-often misunderstanding, new and
far more positive links are now being forged in the chain of
our joint attempts to overcome the past, search together for
meaning in what consistently appears to be meaning-less, and
chart new directions in both Jewish and Christian religious
thinking. Fifty years later, half a century after the most
horrendous events of in all Jewish, Christian, and Western
history, in this instance, Jews and Christians are rejecting
the negative models of the past as contemporary foundations
with which to welcome the future. What will ultimately evolve
as a result of these examinations is, at this very moment,
beyond prediction. Ideally, in some small way, together, we
will have positively contributed to that future. For more
than that, we should not ask.
