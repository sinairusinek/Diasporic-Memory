Most surviving relatives of this family live in Israel, I met one in Buenos Aires and some live
in the U.S. The name "Birnbrey", to the best of my knowledge and belief, survives only
through myself and my children and children's children with the sole exception of Zilla
Birnbrei, however there are no children from that marriage. It appears that her father-in-law
went by the name of Birnbrei. All records in my possession indicate the spelling "Birnbrey".

The known names of those who died as the result of the Holocaust, are shown on a separate
list. However, since my father, Edmund Birnbrey, was killed as the result of Kristallnacht
(Night of the Broken Glass), he probably was the first Holocaust victim in the family.
Kristallnacht took place on November 9, 1938.

My parents are buried on the Jewish cemetery at the Hauptfriedhof in Dortmund (Jüdische
Teil, Feld No. 2, B 12 and B 31). There is a monument to Rachel's family at Alonim and
in 1994 a public memorial was dedicated honoring Martin Gerson in Ahlen, near Hanover,
Germany.
