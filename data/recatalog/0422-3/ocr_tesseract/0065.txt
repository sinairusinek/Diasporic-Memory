In the Middle Ages, the Jews were accused of causing the Black Plague by
poisoning of the wells. It was believed that they continued to torture Jesus Christ
by desecrating his transubstantiated body-the Host wafers. The prevailing view
was that the evilness of the Jews could not be overcome by conversion because
it was in the Jewish blood. The converted Jews were persecuted with the same
vigor. Expulsion was the only solution.
