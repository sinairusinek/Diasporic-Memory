THE JACOBSOHN FAMILY

Virtually all I know about my mother's family are the stories
she told. We lived far away (for pre war German standards) and saw
very little of the family with one exception.

As far as I know the family had lived in Pommern (Pomerania)
for many generations. My grandfather, Theodore, died either in
1934 or 1935 in a home for the aged in Berlin. He and his wife
were in separate hospitals, and unbeknownst to the other, died on
the very same day. My grandfather was in his late nineties and
considering that he had been a photographer all of his life, I have
to assume that he was among the pioneers in that profession.

He was married to Henriette, after whom I was named. They had
ten children and lived in Falkenburg, Pommern and later moved to
Kalis, Pommern. When my grandmother died, my grandfather remarried
and had one more child, Albert Jacobsohn.

My grandfather served in the Prussian Army, and, I understand,
that he had officer rank. He served in the War of 1866 against
Austria and in the Franco-Prussian War of 1870/71.

Three of his sons were killed in World War I (thus my mother
lost three brothers, her husband and child in that War to end all
wars!).

Legend has it, that I visited my grandparents at age one,
unable to.walk, my grandfather handed me a walking cane and off I
‘went and walked from then on. This was also the one and only time
I saw them. |

All of my mother's siblings, with the exception of my mother
and Rosa and Alice's father, settled in Berlin. After my bar
mitzvah in 1936 I visited Berlin and met all of the surviving
brothers and sisters and their families.

The most colorful relative was my Uncle Gustav. He was an
artist and had spent his life painting in Rothenburg and in the
Staedtls of Poland painting Jewish scenes. Before World War I he
was reputed to have painted the Kaiser's children for the Kaiser.
My mother considered him a famous artist, to this date I have been
unable to find his name in any of the books, catalogues and lists
of artists. There also was talk that he had taught at the Academy
of Art in Berlin. He lived on the famous Friedrichstr. My best
memory is that this uncle would like to entertain us by playing a
Singing Saw.
