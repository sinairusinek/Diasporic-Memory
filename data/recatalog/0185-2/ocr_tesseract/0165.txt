From the Desk of Henry Birnbrey

1501 Regency Walk Drive - Decatur, GA 30033

June 19, 1994
Dear Relatives,

I am enclosing herewith the first phase of some of the work
I have been doing on our family genealogy. This is by far
incomplete, however I will continue to make additions and changes
as I go along and obtain further information. Periodically, I hope
to be able to mail you an update to include any additional
information I have.

In the meanwhile, I am asking you to please send me any and
all information which you may have and which is not included in the
material I am enclosing. The program asks for dates and places of
birth, dates and places of marriages and dates and places of death.
You should be able to interpret what parts are missing. Any
additions, corrections and comments will be most appreciated.

In August, in connection with another trip, I am thinking of
going to Stettin for the day. Since I know nothing about Stettin,
nor do I have any contacts there, any suggestions of names and
places will also be appreciated. My travel agent has arranged for
a Jewish guide to meet us there and take us around for the day. If
you know of any relatives who are buried there, and whose graves
are still in tact, such information will also be helpful.

Also please send me all of the known mailing addresses of the
living as I plan to publish that in connection with the finished
work, so that relatives can visit and meet each other, or at least,
when they visit a city, know which relative lives there. My visit
to Buenos Aires was certainly a beautiful experience, which would
not have been possible without this work.

I am also inviting you to make copies of the enclosed material
and distribute it to your family. Some day this material should
answer a lot of questions, if and when the children ask. I know my
children keep asking me questions, and I hope this will answer some
of them. Please take a couple of hours, read this material and see
what additions you can make.

I feel gratified to have been able to bring this work this far
and again I must give credit to Alfons Gerson who really did the
majority of the work, and I felt privileged to be able to use his
material.

When I feel all of the information available has been entered
into this computer program, I plan to “print a typical family tree
which will be easier to follow.
