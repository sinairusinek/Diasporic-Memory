THE KRESSES/COHEN FAMILY

It may appear strange that I am the one to write about Ricky's side of the family, and, of
course, what I write is strictly my remembrances from various conversations and incidents.
Not to take away from anyone else, mother was more interested in her family's history than
the rest of her family and perhaps also kept up with more relatives than anyone else in her
family. Therefore if I stretch a point or fail to report everything accurately, I hope I am
forgiven, as I did my best.

The way 1 understand it, both sides of the family came from Kobrin, Poland. It appears that
most Kobriners settled in Birmingham, Ala. Augusta, Ga. and Chattanooga, Tenn. This was
the result of one or two people making the initial emigration to these communities and then
sending for other family members. There were so many Kobriners in Birmingham that Ricky
always thought that Kobrin surely must have been a big town and only later in life did it
dawn on her that Kobrin was actually a very small stetl in Poland, and to be more specific,

in Grodno Gebernia.

Mother's parents, Hyman and Emma Kresses, were known as Zaide Hymie and Bubbe Emma.
There was good reason for this. When Janet Sugarman was born, both sets of her
grandparents wanted to be known as Bubbe and Zaide, and the only way Janet could properly
identify her grandparents, was to add their first names, and thus the names stuck.

Bubbe Emma's family, the Cohens, came to Birmingham first. It is my understanding that
the Cohens had done very well financially in Birmingham.

Bubbe Emma had a sister named Pesha Mindel Goldfarb who was the mother of Sadie
Courson and Sadie's brother Izzy and the brothers who settled in Chicago (Sam, Abe and
Pinchie). She also had three brothers, Murray, Harry and George (also known as Gabriel).
George passed away at an early age (he was Jay's father). Murray had a shoe store in New
York and later a dry goods business in Tuscaloosa, Ala. He subsequently retired to Miami
Beach. Harry had a business in Birmingham and later in Memphis, Tenn. and he too retired
to Miami Beach. George had a store in North Birmingham and on his death, the store was
sold to Izzy Goldfarb. Murray served in the cavalry during World War I and Harry was the
only member of the family who received some college education and as a matter of fact,
following his retirement to Ft. Lauderdale actually did some teaching at the regional college.

Bubbe Emma was a child when her family arrived in Birmingham, I think pre-school age, she
attended public schools in Birmingham. During World War I she fell in love with a young
gentlemen from a German-Jewish family. This was frowned upon in those days, as if it were
an inter-marriage. In an effort to break up the romance, Bubbe Emma was sent to Cuba
