SPECIAL SHOWING OF "SCHINDLER"
Award Winning Documentary on the
Life of Oskar Schindler

GPM, wpe mr

There will 8 special: showing of the documentary film,
SCHINDLER, introduced by its Director, Jon Blair, on Tuesday night
15th March at 8.00 pm in the Auditorium Maximum.

This 80 minute long film was the winner of a British Academy Award
for Best Documentary in 1983 and features interviews with
survivors who owe their lives to the German businessman, now the
subject of Steven Spielberg's feature film, as well as interviews
with his wife and one of his mistresses. Oskar Schindler himself
is shown briefly in an archive ciip taken shortly before his death

The mistress of Plaszow Forced Labour Camp Commandant, Amon Goeth
talks of Schindler and Goeth in a unique interview given on the
day before she died.

Music is performed by the original camp musicians, Leo-and Henry
Rosner, and the narration is read by Dirk Bogarde, the actor who
gained notoriety for his part as an ex Nazi in "The Night Porter"
and who also was with British troops who liberated Belsen.

The film was used by Spielberg and his actors as one of the
primary sources of research for "Schindler's List".

Mr Blair, who has also spoken with Spielberg extensively about
Schindler, Spielberg's new found Judaism, and the challenges of
making "Schindler's List" on location in Poland, will speak

briefly after the showing and will answer questions.
