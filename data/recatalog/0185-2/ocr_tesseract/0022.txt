It should be noted, that since Bubbe Emma and Zaide Hymie were cousins, the family tree
will list all descendants twice, once as lineal descendants and once as distant cousins, this
is a bit confusing, but I found no other way to program this family tree. This also will
explain the apparent similarity between the lists of the Kresses and Cohen families. Since
we are doubly related we have to be doubly nice to one another!

There always were a number of uncertainties floating around and we often kidded about them.
One of them was the true age of Bubbe Emma and Zaide Hymie. There were conflicting
stories about them and Uncle Harry told me that only he knew the truth but would take it
with him to his grave, which he did. Zaide Hymie used to tell us that an insurance agent
named Sperling (pronounced Shperling) made him a year younger in order to reduce the cost
of his life insurance premium. Consequently all legal documents, citizenship papers, social
security etc. carried this younger age. This never became a problem until Zaide Hymie had
to wait an extra year to become eligible for social security.

The other "mystery" concerned the relationship of one Esther Goldfarb. I used to tease all
members of the family, as each one of them seemed to have a differing explanation of how
Esther fit into the family. I assume when I complete the family tree the truth will emerge.

Zaide Hymie operated a store in North Birmingham and, I assume, during the depression he
gave up or lost the store and moved to Pell City, Alabama where he also had a retail
business. Ricky went to elementary school there, played in the Avondale Mills band and
basically the family was very happy in Pell City, with the exception of the lack of any Jewish
life. Again the family picked up and left and went to Birmingham where Zaide Hymie
opened a shoe department at Berman's Store #3 and several years later the two operations
were merged and he became a partner in that store together with Louis Epstein and Vellye
Berman.
