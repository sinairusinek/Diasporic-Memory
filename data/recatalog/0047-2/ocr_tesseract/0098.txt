Mrs. Landsberg

Very many thanks for your kind letter.

First, I do hope you are quite well again. It
must have been 8 nuisance to be so ill while you
were away, but I trust you are absolutely well again
now. As you said in your letter, I did not let
Dr. Weizmann know, as I felt it better not to worry
him, and you mentioned in your letter also that you
aid not wish me to do so.

Now, I trust you, Dr. Landsberg and the children

are well.

Dr. Weizmann is still away in the Sanatorium x

and this past week or two we have been having some-
what better news. He is making progress, although
it is still somewhat slow, but the physician says
there is no need for anxiety. It will apparently
be two or even three months before he will be per-
mitted to do any work again.
