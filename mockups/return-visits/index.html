Alfred Landsberg — Deutschlandfahrt eines Israeli

An Israeli back in Hamburg, 1951 — nineteen years after leaving

The canonical return-visit text of the corpus: Landsberg leaves Germany in 1932, returns in 1951 for work, reads Hamburg as a foreign city (he never lived there — no memories to disturb), visits his parents' graves in Frankfurt, meets displaced Germans, refuses pity. Followed by companion essays Berlin 1956 and Eine Fahrt nach Ostberlin (~1959).

Twenty cases, one corpus. Every confirmed return-to-Germany case in our holdings is here. Of 228 CJH archival collections swept, exactly one (Ernest Michel) carried a documented return — the theme lives almost entirely in the MTFN / Tefen material. Eleven cases show their key scanned pages right on the card — click any facsimile to enlarge it. The remaining cases — chiefly the rich Otto Mayer family cluster (G-F-0030) — are catalogued but not yet digitised, and are marked scan pending.

Access note. The facsimiles shown here are web copies of the exact pages cited; the small "Drive folder" links lead to the complete scan folders in the project Google Drive and open only for accounts the archive has been shared with.
