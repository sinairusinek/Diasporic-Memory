In my childhood, the belief in ritual murder of children by the Jews was
widespread in Poland. It was commonplace to hear mothers threaten their children
that if they misbehaved, "a Jew would grab them". On July 4, 1946, Holocaust
survivors were brutally murdered by a mob in Kielce, Poland. The massacre was
the result of a rumor that an 8-year-old boy was abducted by the Jews for ritual
murder.
