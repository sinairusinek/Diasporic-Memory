Frida, Ruth and Dani. Start of the WW1.

With the start of the first world war, Max, who was a reserve officer in the Austrian army, was
recruited and leaves the country. Frida Is left home with the children. Frida Is writing: "at night a
british ship is bombing the consulate building in Haifa. Our neighbour, Mr. Keiserman who manage
the A.F.K bank suggests us to leave back to germany. My brother In law Wilhelm Is left to guard the
house and we're back to Europe untill the end of the war.”

In the end of the war, the family returns to Israel. This Is a farewell picture from the Moses-Hecker
family in Frida's home in Germany.
