Szymaniski criticized right-wing nationalists as being superficial and mistaken in laying
all the blame for Poland’s problems at the feet of Jews and Masons. But he did grant the
nationalists at least one point. Even if Jews did not attack Christian culture, they could not be
expected to build it up. Jewish intellectuals were harmful to Poland’s Catholic culture, not at

exponents of some sort of Jewish culture, but precisely as agents of secular culture and a secular

state.
