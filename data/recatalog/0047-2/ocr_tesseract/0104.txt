He was very anxious to see his mother, and
found her in excellent health and spirits.

We expect to leave here about the
9th, and shall be in London about the 18th.
Dr. Weizmann may still stay in Paris a few
days.

Will Dr. Landsberg be coming to the A.C.
meeting in London? I understand it is fixed
for the 19th May.

Am rather rushed here, but feeling well.
Have no news, but wanted to thank you for
your kind letter, and to let you know - as I
realise how pleased it will make you - that
Dr. Weizmann is now much stronger and better.

My warmest greetings to you all. Above
all, I hope you are well.

Yours affectionately,

See mate ANG Neben,

| UERUSALEM.

4 42811 1929.

Your letter dated the 5th was forwarded to

Dear Mrs. Landsberg,

me here. How are you, now? 1.80 2006 you are
well again, and that the family is well too.

I was terribly distressed to hear of your
trouble, and send pr. Landsberg and you, my
deep sympathy.

Dr. Weizmann is now much better thank God.
Michael and I came from London and joined Dr.
and Mrs. Weizmann in Cannes. He was then still
very weak, but is now much stronger, although
he still needs further rest. Helis enjoyed
the Palestine trip and has not done too much,
although it is sometimes difficult to prevent his

overworking.
