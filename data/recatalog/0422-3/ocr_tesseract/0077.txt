Chagal's painting depicts the Eastern European pogroms, where out-of-control
mobs ravaged Jewish communities.

Plank writes, "As the prayer shaw wraps the loins of the crucified figure,
Chagal makes clear that the Christ and the Jewish sufferer are one."

Could it be that Chagal wanted to show that the persecution of the Jews
was taking place in the shadow of the Christian symbol? Plank interprets the
painting differently.
