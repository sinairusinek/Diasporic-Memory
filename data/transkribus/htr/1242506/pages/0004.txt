Sorry to digress, but this is also part of the story. In the and
half of the 19th century, medical diagnosis was based on the doctor's
experience and observation, and less on scientific research as it is
practised nowadays. A country doctor was not too well off. Often he
was paid in kind by the farmers, artisans, and workers he was
treating, as these had no cash either. Therefore a microscope was a
luxury, Mrs. Koch probably saved up for this birthday present for a
long time, or maybe she did some work of some sort, until she had
enough money to pay for this precious instrument. Those were the days
when the average person worked hard, earned little, was modest and
mostly happy with his lot. Nowadays the average person is much better
off, and strangely enough not very happy with his lot
