As I pointed out, three brothers were killed in World War I.
One of these was Emil Jacobsohn the father of Rosa and Alice.
Emil's wife, Jette, was the sister of Georg Fuchs who, because he
was my G-dfather, became my closest relative. All of us lived in
the same apartment building in Dortmund on Friedrich St. 13. When
my mother's daughter Ruth was suffering from malnutrition during
the war, my aunt invited my mother, who was then living in
Frankfurt, to live with her in Dortmund because she, my aunt, had
a cow and thereby could provide milk for the child. However the
child did die at a young age, I think age 2 or 3.

On this basis I have recorded ten brothers and sisters and I
simply have no recall on who is #11.

One of the other brothers killed in World War I (I regret I
cannot recall which one) was married to Rosel who was a sister of
Martha Strauss. Martha Strauss was the mother of Walter and Henry
Strauss. Aunt Rosel remarried and also lived in Dortmund and we
did maintain some relationship with her. When I left for the U.S.
she made sure that I would contact her nephew Walter, who was then
living in Bluefield, W.Va.

The known names of those who perished in the Holocaust are
recorded elsewhere.
