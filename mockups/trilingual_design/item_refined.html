Anti Anti — Fact on the Jewish Question

Communication Document · 1934 · Print · German + Hebrew

Folder with cards on topics of the 'Jewish Question' (antisemitism, etc.), published by the Central Association of German Citizens of the Jewish Faith, published by 'Philo' Berlin, with the title 'Anti Anti'.

Anti Anti — Tatsache zur Judenfrage. Herausgegeben und verlegt vom Centralverein deutscher Staatsbürger jüd. Glaubens E.V. Berlin.

Reference of the Association of German Citizens of the Jewish Faith to the request of René Weiss from 1934 in connection with the revocation of his German citizenship.
