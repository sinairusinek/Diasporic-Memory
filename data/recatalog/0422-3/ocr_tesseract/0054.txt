B: General

The founding fathers of the United States made a great contribution to the
viability of the country by creating a barrier between Church and State. Political
power in the hands of true believers inevitably leads to tyranny and persecution.
Constantine the Great established Christianity as the state religion of the Roman
Empire in the fourth century. This was spiritually not beneficial to Christianity and
proved disastrous for the Jews. Combining the cross with a sword was not a good
idea. Even religion becomes corrupted by power.

Antisemitism has three components:

1 Emotions
2 Beliefs
3 Power

Fear of strangers (xenophobia) is a primitive and universal emotion. It may
give rise to an elaborate belief system of secular or religious nature. A belief

system combined with power often leads to persecution of the non-believers.
