Hecker Family

This week(Feb 27, 2007) Ruth (Hecker) Horan will be 100 years old. Despite her age, her memory
Is clear and she can read books without glasses and look at her family album. Clearly she remembers
all the stories behind the pictures, and we get a short look at those moments of Israel in the begining
of the 20th century.

Her story begins in Germany and flows to Kibbutz ‏יווצ‎ at in Yizra* el Vally.

Max Mordechai Hecker was born in 1877 to a jewish family in Hamburg. He studies civil engineering
and join a zionist student movement. He meets Frida Moses and join the Austrian army as soon as he
graduate.

With the start of the first world war, Max, who was a reserve officer in the Austrian army, was
recruited and leaves the country. Frida Is left home with the children. Frida Is writing: "at night a
british ship is bombing the consulate building in Haifa. Our neighbour, Mr. Keiserman who manage
the A.F.K bank suggests us to leave back to germany. My brother In law Wilhelm Is left to guard the
house and we're back to Europe untill the end of the war.”

In Binyamina's "Meshek HaPoalot" (farm based on women's labor) Ruth Hecker met her future
husband, David Horan. The two contribute greatly to the new face of Israel. David is working on the
new water line "Kav HaShiloach", while Ruth joins the Sharon group (later to be called Kibbutz Ramat
David). Here are moments from the couple's life in early Israel.

In 1931 Max gets a job as an In the | y of Jerusalem, and plans the draining
system of the city. Along with this he keep doing what he like most, Eretz Yisrael archeology.

A decade later, Max and frieda, who change their name to Moderchal and Chana, move to live with
their daughter Ruth, in Kibbutz Ramat David, and later in 1952 they move with her to Kibbitz Yifat.
Mordechai finds a Roman tomb in the kibbutz, research a Roman road near Zippori and more. Chana
keeps painting the views of Israel and its folks. In 1964, Max died in the age of 87, and ten years later
his wife passes away in the age of 93.
