Givat-Brenner          29. 10. 96.


Dear Judith and Gideon!
To avoid being mistaken with names I am writing to
You in English. It concerns the questions of Henry Birnbrey
to whom I am sending my regards.

I don't remember any Angemündstreet .
What I know for shure, is, that Hugo Gerson, his
wife Rosa (born Bittermann) and their children lived
many years in the town " Angermünde ".
Later I heard, that they were deportet together to
Poland. After the war my brother Alfons investigated
about their fate, but to no avail.

Ruth Diergardt and Stephan got safely and dry to the
airport and through all the examinations. And this, while
there had been rain in Neve-Mivtach and in Javne.

Among my old photos I found one of Mrs. Wormser,
Your grandmother. In 1936, when on vacation with our
father in Frankfurt, Imade some tripps to the environs.
After Heidelberg I went to Dürkheim, to pay her a visit.
The other I made 1938 in Notea - Boneh. Perhaps You
have it yet.
Keep well, and so all the family!

Yours
[handschriftlich]
