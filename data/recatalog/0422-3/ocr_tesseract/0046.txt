ANTISEMITISM, CHRISTIANITY AND THE HOLOCAUST
Antisemitism, like love, can be described but it is impossible to explain it
fully. Whenever | hear the word "because" combined with antisemitism, | suspect
that the speaker has a propagandistic agenda. Propaganda provides pseudo-
explanations in order to produce attitudes and actions.

"There was antisemitism in Poland because Jews were Communists."

"There was antisemitism in Russian because Jews were Capitalists."

"Poles are anti-Semites because they suck antisemitism with their mother's
milk." "Poles are anti-Semites because the Catholic Church promoted the hatred
of Jews for hundreds of years."

These statements are not explanations but excuses or accusations.

It is difficult for Americans to understand European antisemitism as there
never was true antisemitism in the U.S.A.

Antisemitism was a social movement and a political force in Europe.

Antisemitism should not be confused with bias, prejudice or dislike of Jews.
